Project 03 / AI for the field

FieldLine

AI for bridging the communication gap effectively between the project lead and the field workers in large infrastructure projects. Aids project lead to manage the overall project effectively, while also guiding the execution team on the field.

Visit FieldLine
Read project note
Purpose

Connects project schedules with on-site reports and work updates, helping teams see what is completed, delayed, or at risk, while autonomously updating project status, and warning managers / admin only when any kind of anomalities are detected.

Engineering pov

Core AI + Applied AI : Houses machine learning models trained specifically for anomaly detection (statistical) and progress classification (logistic regression) in the progresses reported by the field workers, while appiled AI is used in usual AI response generaton, image recognition, while also allowing workers to report in their native language through speech to speech.

Current state

FieldLine v1 is stable and has been deployed. Initially developed for SIH 2026. Qualified in Round 1. Round 2 is under evaluation.

01 / 03

SpecialisationBuilt for infrastructure projects that need reliable progress tracking and early identification of delays.
Field ContextBring together daily reports, site records, spreadsheets, and inspection documents.
Easy ReportingAllows non-tech savvy field workers to report their progresses easily through text / speech in native language.
Linguistic barriersConverts the report made in native lanugage by the worker into english, hence addressing linguistic barries between the admin and the execution team.
